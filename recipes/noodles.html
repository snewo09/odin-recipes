Instant Noodles

Description

When I was in college, there were occasions when I would spend an entire evening working on a video editing project, only to realize at some unreasonable hour that I hadn't eaten dinner. Instant noodles were a particularly convenient solution to this problem. They were cheap, required virtually no culinary knowledge, and could be prepared in approximately the amount of time it took Premiere to render something. Were they nutritionally sufficient? Debatable. But that wasn't necessarily my primary concern as a college student.

I think instant noodles get an unnecessarily bad reputation as a food exclusively for college students and people who have given up on cooking. The beauty of instant noodles is that they require very little effort to improve upon. Adding an egg, some green onions, and a bit of chili oil can make for a surprisingly satisfying meal without substantially increasing the amount of work involved. Of course, you can also just follow the instructions on the package. I'm not going to pretend that every meal needs to be a creative endeavor.

Ingredients

  • 1 package of instant ramen noodles
  • 1 included seasoning packet
  • 2 cups water
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 green onion, sliced
  • 1 teaspoon chili oil

Steps

  1. Bring 2 cups of water to a boil in a small saucepan.
  2. Add the noodles and seasoning packet, then crack the egg into the broth. Cook until the noodles are tender and the egg white and yolk are set.
  3. Transfer to a bowl, garnish with sliced green onions, and drizzle with chili oil.
